What this shows that the target view alone cannot

  1. The same image identity runs the whole length. checkout-api:2.7.0 is built in wave 0, lands in a registry, is scanned by digest, then appears unchanged on both stage targets. That continuity is the thing a pipeline view is for — and the reason digestMatch is drawn rather than assumed.
  2. Sources are plural and asymmetric. One repo drives two Types (code→image, chart/**→chart) and a second repo drives the database. Three sources, one service — a single "repo" field could not say this.
  3. Scan sits between build and stage, not inside a wave. It is commander-side and runs once per promotion journey over the artifact digest, so it belongs on the connector, not on a target.
  4. Infrastructure precedes configuration inside a wave. The database is created before the workload that needs it — visible as ordering within the wave rather than as two unrelated rows.
  5. Bake is declared but not started, shown as a dashed chip rather than omitted. An unstarted bake and an absent one are different facts.
Fixture. No such pipeline exists on your estate — you have one releases_via edge, zero registries and zero source mappings. This is the shape a K8s microservice would take, not a reading of your data.
