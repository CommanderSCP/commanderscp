Container · Dock · Warehouse

All three ship in lucide. Shown large, then on a size ramp down to 14px, then against your family.

Container
32 24 18 14
A shipping container, drawn isometric. Strongest logistics read of the three, and the ribs give it a silhouette nothing else in your set has. But it is the only isometric mark you would own — guidon, stack, crate and reticle are all flat elevation. At 14px the interior ribs collapse into noise.
Dock
32 24 18 14
A loading dock — a bay with a header band and a sill. Three strokes, flat, survives 14px intact, and it is semantically exact: the dock is where materiel leaves from. Matches your family's construction. Least distinctive silhouette — a banded rectangle. Beside the crate it can read as another box.
Warehouse
32 24 18 14
A depot shed: shallow pitched shell, roller door, two shelf lines. The unambiguous "depot" of the three — nobody mistakes it for anything else, and its roof is far shallower than the house I drew. It is still a building. If the house shape was the objection, this is the same objection with better proportions.
